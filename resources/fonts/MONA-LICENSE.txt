Why this font
-------------
KiriKiri/KAG games request Windows face names ("ＭＳ ゴシック" / "ＭＳ 明朝",
MS PGothic/MS Gothic). Mona is a metric-compatible clone of MS PGothic —
designed so Shift-JIS art and text laid out for MS PGothic render identically —
so it is the closest open substitute for the games' intended typeface. It covers
kana and kanji (7,211 glyphs in cmap, including U+3042/30A2/4E00/65E5/6F22).
